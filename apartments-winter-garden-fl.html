Why Renters Are Moving to Winter Garden

Winter Garden has grown quickly as a rental market in the Orlando metro. The Horizons West and Hamlin areas along the SR-429 corridor now have a large and growing number of newer apartment units, most built in recent years. Streets are wider, schools are newer, and access to the theme parks is closer than most people expect.

For renters, Winter Garden checks a lot of boxes: newer construction, quieter residential character, and quick highway access via SR-429, I-4, and the Florida Turnpike. Disney World and Universal are a short drive from most buildings in the corridor via SR-429.

It also works well if you work in Windermere, Dr. Phillips, or southwest Orlando and want to cut your commute without paying premium rent in those zip codes.

What Apartments Look Like in Winter Garden

The Horizons West and Hamlin corridor has a large selection of newer apartment communities, most built between 2017 and 2024. You will find a mix of mid-rise and garden-style buildings, typically with resort amenities like pools, fitness centers, dog parks, and coworking spaces.

Which specific Winter Garden communities I can place you in changes over time as leasing partnerships shift, so I'll give you the current picture directly when you reach out rather than posting a price here that might be stale by the time you read it. Building age and distance from the Hamlin retail core are the two biggest levers on price in this corridor, more than anywhere else in the metro I cover.

If I can't help in Winter Garden itself when you reach out, I'll say so plainly and tell you whether a nearby area such as Dr. Phillips, Lake Nona, Davenport or Kissimmee fits your budget better. If you are comparing Winter Garden to Kissimmee or Davenport, expect to pay somewhat more here. What you get in return is newer construction, better roads, and schools with strong ratings by local reputation in Orange County.

Great Location for Disney and Theme Park Employees

Winter Garden is a good fit for Walt Disney World cast members and employees who want a residential neighborhood without living on the tourist corridor. You get distance from US-192 and the congestion around it while staying within easy commuting range of property.

SR-429 connects directly to the US-192 and SR-535 interchange near Disney, so the drive is mostly highway with few traffic lights through the residential stretch. Most cast members living here report a manageable commute during standard shift hours.

If you want to compare areas closer to the parks, Davenport and Kissimmee are worth a look, and I can tell you whether I can help there too. Just let me know your work location and I will factor commute time into your shortlist.
