Who I Am

I'm Willy Lopez, a licensed Florida real estate agent based in Marion County. I work with buyers and sellers in Ocala and Marion Oaks directly, and I help renters find an apartment across Greater Orlando and Ocala.

I'm bilingual, English and Spanish, and I talk to clients in whichever one they're more comfortable in. A lot of the people I work with are navigating a first home purchase, a move to Florida, or a rental they're weighing whether to keep or sell, and I'd rather explain things plainly than talk over anyone.

Before real estate, my background is in operations, including time at Costco. That's where I learned to build a process, follow through on it, and fix it when it doesn't work. I bring the same approach to a home search or a listing: a clear next step, every time, instead of leaving you guessing what happens next.

How I Work

I respond to every inquiry within 24 business hours. I'll give you a straight answer, including when I don't think a property, a program, or a decision is the right fit for you.

For buyers, I represent you directly in Ocala and Marion Oaks, and I help first-time buyers understand programs like Florida Hometown Heroes before they commit to anything. For sellers, I give you an honest read on pricing and timing rather than the number that gets me a listing. For landlords, I place a qualified tenant in a Marion County rental for one month's rent, owed only when the lease is signed. I also run a separate apartment locating service for renters across Greater Orlando and Ocala, free to the renter because the community pays the fee.

Wherever you are in the process, buying your first home, deciding whether to rent out or sell a property, or looking for an apartment, reach out and I'll tell you plainly what your options look like.
