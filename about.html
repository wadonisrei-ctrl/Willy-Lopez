Who I Am

I'm Willy Lopez, a licensed Florida real estate agent based in Ocala. I represent buyers and sellers anywhere in Florida, and the areas I know best are Ocala and Marion Oaks. I also place tenants for landlords in Marion County and help renters in Greater Orlando and Ocala.

I'm bilingual, English and Spanish, and I talk to clients in whichever one they're more comfortable in. A lot of the people I work with are navigating a first home purchase, a move to Florida, or a rental they're weighing whether to keep or sell, and I'd rather explain things plainly than talk over anyone.

For buyers, I handle the research, schedule every showing, and manage the back-and-forth with listing agents, and I'll tell you plainly when a property isn't right for you. For sellers, I give you a straight read on your home's value and won't push you to list before it makes sense. I respond to every inquiry within 24 business hours and stay with you through closing.

How I Work

I respond to every inquiry within 24 business hours. I'll give you a straight answer, including when I don't think a property, a program, or a decision is the right fit for you.

For buyers, I represent you anywhere in Florida, and I help first-time buyers understand programs like Florida Hometown Heroes before they commit to anything. For sellers, I give you an honest read on pricing and timing rather than the number that gets me a listing. For landlords, I place a qualified tenant in a Marion County rental for one month's rent, owed only when the lease is signed. I also run a separate apartment locating service for renters across Greater Orlando and Ocala, free to the renter because the community pays the fee.

Wherever you are in the process, buying your first home, deciding whether to rent out or sell a property, or looking for an apartment, reach out and I'll tell you plainly what your options look like.
