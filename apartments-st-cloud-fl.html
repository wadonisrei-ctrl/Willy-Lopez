Why Renters Are Choosing St. Cloud

St. Cloud sits in south Osceola County, about 30 miles southeast of downtown Orlando and 10 miles east of Kissimmee. It borders Lake Tohopekaliga and has a small-town, lakefront character that is rare in the Orlando metro.

For renters, St. Cloud is generally one of the more affordable areas in the region. You typically get more space for your dollar compared to Lake Nona, Kissimmee, or downtown Orlando. The Florida Turnpike runs nearby, which makes it a workable base for people who drive to jobs in Kissimmee, Orlando, or even Brevard County.

It is a strong fit for families who want space and a quieter setting, for outdoor enthusiasts who spend time on the water, and for renters who have been priced out of the markets closer to the city core.

Good Fit for Families and Outdoor Renters

St. Cloud suits you well if you spend time outdoors. Lake Tohopekaliga is a well-known bass fishing lake with public boat ramps, parks, and a small lakefront area with restaurants and a marina. It is a real amenity for people who want access to open water without paying lake-view pricing.

For families, St. Cloud offers Osceola County schools and a lower-density residential feel. The neighborhoods are more established than the newer planned communities farther north in the county.

If you are comparing St. Cloud to Kissimmee, the main difference is that St. Cloud is generally quieter, more affordable, and farther from the tourist corridor. Whether that is a benefit depends on what you are looking for.

Commuting from St. Cloud

The Florida Turnpike is the main artery for St. Cloud commuters. It connects north to Kissimmee and Orlando and is the primary route for residents who work in the broader metro.

Downtown Orlando is a straight run up the Turnpike in normal traffic. Kissimmee and the theme park area are closer still. Brevard County is reachable the same way, which makes St. Cloud one of the few areas in Osceola where a Space Coast commute is realistic.

If commute time is a factor in your decision, give me your work location when you reach out and I will factor it into the shortlist I build for you.
